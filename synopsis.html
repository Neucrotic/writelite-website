Community

WriteLite has a Discord Community Server – a place for writers of all genres, styles and experience levels to gather, share, and discuss the craft of writing. Competitions will be hosted in the future.

The WriteLite Instagram page is a great place to keep track of updates, connect with other writers or share snippets of your work.

These are both places where you can reach out to the developer directly with questions, suggestions and feedback.

Support

WriteLite is free and will stay that way.

If it has become part of how you write — if it opens fast when you need it to, stays out of your way when you are in flow, and keeps your files where they belong — a small donation is a meaningful way to say so.

It goes directly toward keeping the app maintained, updated, and bug free.

If you are able to, thank you. If not, keep writing.
